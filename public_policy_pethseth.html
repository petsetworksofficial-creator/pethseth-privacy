Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2, 2026

Welcome to Pethseth. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us while using our application.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it is stored, and how you can contact us regarding your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Pethseth, we may collect:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, mobile number and user account ID.
  • Account Information: Login credentials and information associated with your account.
  • Location Information: Your device's location, when you grant location permission, to help find nearby mess services.
  • Vendor Information: Mess name, contact information, mess location and other details provided by vendors.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and provide access to the app.
  • Display available mess services.
  • Calculate distances between users and mess locations.
  • Allow vendors to manage their mess information.
  • Maintain and improve the functionality of Pethseth.
  • Respond to user questions and support requests.

3. Location Permissions

Pethseth may request access to your device's location to help identify nearby mess services and calculate distances.

Location access is requested through Android's permission system. You can control or revoke this permission through your device settings.

Vendors manually provide the location of their mess through the application's map selection feature.

4. Data Storage and Security

Pethseth uses a Node.js backend hosted on Render and a TiDB database to store and manage application information.

User passwords are hashed before being stored. We implement reasonable security measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by hosting, database and infrastructure providers that help us operate Pethseth. Such providers process information as necessary to provide their services.

6. Third-Party Services

Pethseth uses third-party infrastructure and mapping technologies to provide its services.

Our backend is hosted on Render and our database is provided by TiDB.

Pethseth uses MapLibre for map functionality. Map data and map tiles may be provided by external services, which may process technical information such as IP addresses when map resources are requested.

We do not currently use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services, maintain user accounts and fulfil applicable legal obligations.

Users who wish to request deletion of their account and associated personal information can contact us at:

petsetworks.official@gmail.com

8. Children's Privacy

Pethseth is primarily designed for university students, individuals looking for mess services, and mess vendors. The application is not restricted exclusively to users aged 18 or above.

We recognize that individuals under the age of 18 may use the application. We handle personal information belonging to children in accordance with applicable data protection and privacy laws.

Where applicable law requires verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian before processing a child's personal information, we will obtain that consent before carrying out such processing.

We do not knowingly use children's personal information for purposes prohibited by applicable law.

If you are a parent or lawful guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information to us, you can contact us at petsetworks.official@gmail.com .
